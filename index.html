Keep scrolling and watch the house get built.

Phase 1 of 6

Foundation

Grid, levels and soil conditions are checked before a single footing is cast. Getting this right keeps every later stage square.

Phase 2 of 6

Structure

Columns, beams and slabs go up to the structural drawing. Reinforcement is inspected before each pour, not after.

Phase 3 of 6

Masonry

Walls rise to line and level, so doors, windows and finishes fit cleanly and nothing needs patching later.

Phase 4 of 6

Openings and services

Window frames, electrical conduits and plumbing lines are fixed before finishing starts, so no finished wall gets cut open.

Phase 5 of 6

Interiors

Ceilings, woodwork, lighting and paint are done by the team that built the shell, with one engineer answerable for the whole job.

Phase 6 of 6

Handover

A joint walkthrough, a snag list closed out, and the final drawings and documents handed to you.
