What we take on

From initial geotechnical analysis to turnkey structural handover, we deliver engineered durability, transparent material billing, and certified structural safety.

IS 456:2000 and IS 1893 compliant

End-to-end execution of residential (G+1, G+2, duplex) and commercial RCC structures with strict adherence to Indian Standard structural codes.

  • Geotechnical soil testing and load-bearing foundation design
  • M25/M30 grade concrete with Fe500D TMT anti-seismic reinforcement
  • Precision column alignment and vibrated roof slab casting
  • High-density AAC blockwork and weather-shield thermal exterior plaster
  • Complete electrical conduit piping and high-pressure plumbing installation
